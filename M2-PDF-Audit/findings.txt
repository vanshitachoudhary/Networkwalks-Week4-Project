M2 - PDF ENCRYPTION AUDIT

Environment: Isolated local practice lab
Tool: qpdf
Test Files: 3 synthetic PDFs

Initial Assessment:
- sample1.pdf - Not encrypted
- sample2.pdf - Not encrypted
- sample3.pdf - Not encrypted

Encryption Applied:
All three sample PDFs were protected using AES-256 (AESv3).

Verification:
- All three encrypted PDFs accepted the configured test password.
- Encryption revision: R = 6
- Encryption method: AESv3

Observation:
Printing, extraction and modification permissions
were allowed in the test configuration.

Conclusion:
AES-256 encryption was successfully applied
and verified on all three synthetic PDFs.

Safety Note:
No real patient PDFs or confidential records were accessed.
